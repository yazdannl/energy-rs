1. **High — M7, capacity-learning INTENDED:** The learning contract is not numerically enforceable. “Long SoC spans” and “quantisation uncertainty” are undefined, and label pooling lacks eligibility, weighting and self-exclusion rules. No absolute capacity bounds or complete aging-band behavior are specified. This permits inflated confidence or legitimate estimates being permanently rejected. **Required change:** Define these calculations and bounds, including fallback behavior and how fresh evidence can overcome a stale prior or aging guard.

2. **High — M7, stall-recovery INTENDED:** Re-extracting open runs does not fully prevent another silent stall. The contract does not define restart recovery, completion detection, or when extraction/learning progress may advance after errors. Advancing the checkpoint too early could recreate the reported failure. **Required change:** Specify durable extraction and per-pack consumption checkpoints; advance them only after the corresponding results are persisted, with idempotent retry and restart behavior.

3. **High — M7, diagnostics INTENDED:** The proposed fields cannot reliably distinguish “attempted but learned nothing” from “learned.” Named blockers cover only three session-availability cases, not invalid samples, confidence rejection, bound rejection, unchanged estimates, extraction failures or persistence failures. **Required change:** Publish an explicit per-pack attempt outcome, separate capacity/efficiency results, accepted/rejected sample counts and reasons on every due or manual attempt—including failures. Define whether manual relearn bypasses the “new completed session” gate.

4. **High — M7, efficiency CURRENT / missing INTENDED contract:** Pairing thresholds are listed, but sample ownership and invalid-data boundaries are unspecified. Reusing blocks, pairing across refused direction changes, or treating correlated pairs as independent could produce false confidence. **Required change:** Define block construction, refusal barriers, pairing order, reuse policy, effective-pair calculation and physical efficiency bounds. State explicitly that capacity and efficiency eligibility cannot veto each other, and define recovery from each guard.

5. **High — M7, `updatedAt` INTENDED:** “Last real measurement” is ambiguous for pooled capacity, multi-session efficiency and manual replay. A pass can consume old data without producing a new accepted measurement; capacity can also succeed while efficiency is blocked. **Required change:** Define accepted-measurement identity and timestamp provenance separately for capacity and efficiency. Blocked, failed and identical-input replay passes must preserve measurement timestamps; attempts and persistence times need separate fields.

6. **Medium — M7, full-charge maintenance:** Partial fills correctly avoid dwell and completion stamps, but their continuation is unspecified. The contract also does not define observed full-charge qualification, interrupted dwell behavior, or recovery after completion persistence fails. This can leave maintenance overdue indefinitely or falsely credit a completed hold. **Required change:** Define due-time anchoring, partial-fill retry/carryover, telemetry-based target and dwell qualification, interruption rules, and idempotent persistence recovery. Preserve import-ceiling protection throughout; advisory/dry-run must not be a recovery mechanism.

7. **Medium — M7, acceptance conditions:** The intended fixes lack concrete regression acceptance criteria. **Required change:** Require tests covering the reported multi-hour stall, restart and persistence failures, exhaustive attempt outcomes, shallow-session confidence, stale-prior recovery, invalid efficiency boundaries, partial-fill continuation and unchanged timestamps on blocked/replayed passes—all while the engine remains active.

**Verdict: approve with required changes (1–7).**
